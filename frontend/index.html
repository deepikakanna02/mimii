      What happens to a recording

      1. Resample and trimMixed to mono at 16 kHz, cut or padded to 10 seconds
      2. Mel spectrogram128 bands by 313 frames, normalised with training statistics
      3. Two autoencodersEach tries to rebuild the spectrogram of a healthy pump
      4. Error featuresMean, max, spread and 90th percentile of the rebuild error
      5. Classifier voteThree classifiers vote, weighted by validation AUC
